![Spinosaurus reconstruction]({{site.baseurl}}/img/spinosaurus.png)

*Spinosaurus* lived in North Africa about 99 to 93.5 million years ago. Its long, narrow jaws and cone-shaped teeth were suited to catching fish, and tall spines rising from its back formed a distinctive sail.

At an estimated 14 m long, *Spinosaurus* was among the longest known carnivorous dinosaurs. Scientists have found only incomplete skeletons, so details of its size, appearance, and how it moved are still being studied.
